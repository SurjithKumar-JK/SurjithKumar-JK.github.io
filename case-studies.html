Internal project case studies

Six selected projects from my engineering experience across computer vision, railway inspection, industrial AI and intelligent transportation. Descriptions are intentionally sanitized for public presentation.

Case study 01 · Computer vision · Privacy

Adaptive Human Face Anonymization – Fisheye & Wide-Angle Vision

Challenge: Apply face detection and anonymization in fisheye and wide-angle imagery, where image distortion and temporal consistency are important engineering considerations.

Approach: Engineered a three-stage pipeline covering face detection, temporal intelligence and rendering. The workflow used RetinaFace with TensorFlow and PyTorch, with fisheye-aware adaptive thresholding, hysteresis filtering and temporal tracking.

Engineering focus: Multi-stage computer vision, temporal processing and GPU-based execution using NVIDIA H100 in the project environment.

RetinaFaceTensorFlowPyTorchNVIDIA H100
Case study 02 · UAV · Railway inspection

UAV-Based Railway Infrastructure Inspection

Challenge: Analyse UAV-captured railway imagery and connect image-level detections with geographic context.

Approach: Developed YOLOv11-based defect-detection solutions for aerial railway imagery. Engineered a C++ pixel-to-geographic coordinate transformation workflow using GPS telemetry, flight altitude and camera orientation to localize image features.

Engineering focus: Object detection, photogrammetry-related inputs and geospatial localization.

YOLOv11C++GISGPSPhotogrammetry
Case study 03 · Automation · Image processing

Automated Image Preprocessing for Railway Inspection

Challenge: Modernize a legacy image-processing workflow and reduce manual preparation of railway inspection imagery.

Approach: Developed a Dockerized Linux command-line application integrating Phase One SDK capabilities for image enhancement, noise reduction and nadir/oblique classification. Built an automated workflow to convert raw IIQ imagery into ML-ready datasets.

Engineering focus: C++ application development, SDK integration, Linux automation and data preparation.

C++DockerLinuxPhase One SDK

Case study 06 · Research · Railway computer vision

Segmentation of Overlapping Ballast Coverage on Wooden Railway Sleepers

Challenge: Address overlapping ballast coverage in railway sleeper imagery using a computer-vision approach.

Approach: Worked on a transfer-learning-based segmentation approach for overlapping ballast coverage on wooden railway sleepers.

Recognition: The work was selected for poster presentation under the Demand-Based Technology Interventions category at the International Rail Safety Council 2023 in Cape Town, South Africa.

Engineering focus: Transfer learning, image segmentation and railway inspection imagery.
